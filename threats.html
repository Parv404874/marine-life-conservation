⚠️ Threats to Marine Life

Marine ecosystems face several threats caused by human activities and environmental changes. These threats can damage ocean habitats and put marine species at risk.

🗑️ Plastic Pollution

Plastic waste can enter oceans and harm marine animals. It can also pollute beaches and marine habitats.

🎣 Overfishing

Excessive fishing can reduce fish populations and disturb the natural balance of marine ecosystems.

🌡️ Climate Change

Rising ocean temperatures and changing ocean conditions can affect marine species and coral reefs.

🪸 Habitat Destruction

Damage to coral reefs, mangroves and other marine habitats can reduce safe places for marine species to live.
